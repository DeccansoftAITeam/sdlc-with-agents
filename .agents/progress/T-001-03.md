## Decisions
- Ephemeral signing key only when environment=local; any other environment requires `jwt_private_key_pem` (Key Vault in M9)
- Family revocation is committed BEFORE the 401 (raise after the transaction closes)
- Rate limits are in-process (one replica in v1); must move to Postgres/Redis before scaling out (M10 note)
- Client IP = request.client.host. Behind Next.js/ACA this needs X-Forwarded-For handling from a trusted proxy (M10 note)
- Access tokens aren't re-checked against `is_active` for their 15-minute life (stateless JWT trade-off, ADR-0002)
- TD-001/AC-10 deferred (no reset links in v1); signup limit closes TM-013
