## Decisions
- Policy rows are created lazily: a tenant with no row uses `DEFAULT_POLICY` (constitution §8). Satisfies AC-1 without touching signup (auth/** is out of scope)
- Targets are stored in **minutes of counted time**; "1 business day" = 540 min (the default 09:00–18:00 day). Admins edit minutes
- Each ticket snapshots the policy (`tickets.sla_policy`), so policy edits never move existing deadlines (AC-2)
- Pauses are a JSONB list on the ticket (`paused_intervals`), not a separate table: one ticket, few pauses, always read together
- While a ticket is `pending_customer`, `resolution_due_at` is NULL (clock stopped); it is recomputed when the pause closes
- Reopen (resolved → open) recomputes but does not restart: deadlines always count from creation (constitution §8)
- Every assignment notifies (unique dedupe key per assignment); TD-007 uses stable `breach:{ticket}:{type}` keys
- Notification retention is per tenant (`purge_expired(tenant_id)`); the loop over tenants comes with TD-007's job runner (ADR-0003 per-tenant loops)
- `member` dependency moved to `users/deps.py` (planned follow-up from PR C, needed by a second feature now)
