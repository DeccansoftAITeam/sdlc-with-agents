# T-008-02: Migration, masking, budget

**Objective:** the TD-008 tables, PII masking (AC-4) and per-tenant AI limits (AC-6).

## Done
- Migration `td008a` adds `ai_triage_jobs`, `ai_suggestions` and `ai_usage`, each with forced RLS. It's additive only.
- `masking.py` masks emails, card-like numbers (13–19 digits) and phone numbers (7–15 digits), and keeps short numbers such as order ids.
- Hypothesis found a real gap: valid emails can contain `/`, `!` and similar characters in the local part (`/@a.ac`). The pattern now covers every RFC 5322 atext character, and five more seeds pass.
- `budget.py`:
  - `reserve` refuses when the month's 200k tokens are spent or the tenant made 20 calls in the last minute
  - `record` upserts the provider-reported usage
  - the month is the calendar month in UTC
- Tests cover the cap, the rate limit and per-tenant isolation through RLS.

## Decisions
- The rate limit uses the existing in-process limiter. The worker runs one replica, so that's enough (the same caveat as login limits, M10).
- One in-flight call can overshoot the monthly cap. The next call is refused, which the ADR accepts.
